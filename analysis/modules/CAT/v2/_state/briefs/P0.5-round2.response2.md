## Dialogue round 2: answers to the open proposals

DECISION: Catalogue-content stories with no dedicated policy trace to POL-CAT-001
Accepted with one amendment. The TRACES RULE needs at least one POL, and engine §3 makes "the [role] needs to manage [entity]" a legitimate seed. Option B would leave six of the nine registry entities without a story, so P1 would have nothing to derive REQs from. Each story names the channel it serves, so the trace to the one-catalogue policy is honest.
The amendment concerns sourcing. US-CAT-009, -015 and -018 rest on module-registry AUTO-decisions, which the user has not confirmed. Their Source lines now say so, and the PRD carries a note for the approver. If the approver rejects one, the story is struck at the gate, not edited.

DECISION: Story priority is given only where the knowledge base states or clearly implies it
Accepted. Option A would discard real signal in §4.2 and §8. The rule is applied the same way to every story:
- **HIGH:** the §6 simplicity requirements (made measurable by D9), the fixed decision D2, the §2 e-invoicing driver, the differentiator of §1, and variants and bundles (required by the first two launch presets in §4.2).
- **MEDIUM:** storefront-facing stories, because the storefront goes live in wave 2 (§8.2).
- **LOW:** weighed items, because produce and meat by weight is not in the launch priority of §4.2.
- **"—":** everything else.
Priority orders attention inside CAT only. Per D10, CAT ships whole in wave 1.
traces: POL-CAT-001, POL-CAT-002, POL-CAT-003, POL-CAT-004, POL-CAT-005, POL-CAT-006, POL-CAT-007, POL-CAT-008, POL-CAT-009

DECISION: The item-name language rule is a NEED story, and the rule itself goes to P1
Accepted. Registry OI-12 says the rule is applied by P0.5 as a standard language rule and is not a client-specific POL. Engine §8 forbids field-level constraints in the PRD. So US-CAT-008 states the need (Arabic name, brand or model text as written, optional English name). The rule "Arabic name mandatory, English optional; the Arabic name may contain Latin brand or model text" is recorded in RESOLVED DECISIONS for P1 to cite. The legal basis is KSA VAT Implementing Regulations Art. 53, which requires the tax invoice in Arabic.
traces: POL-CAT-006, POL-CAT-001, US-CAT-008

DECISION: Standard values of the lookup `einvoice-item-code-type` are GS1 and EGS
Accepted. The registry delegated these values to P0.5 from the ETA source. The Egypt ETA e-invoice SDK `itemType` takes `GS1` or `EGS`. ZATCA's UBL carries a standard item identifier (GTIN) with no separate type list, and the Variant barcode already covers that identifier. The values are mdl lookup values, so they receive no ID.
traces: POL-CAT-001, US-CAT-021

DECISION: Catalogue change history and catalogue permissions get no story
Accepted. Permissions for the three preset roles (KB D8) are a P1 permission matrix (engine §8). Change history is behaviour of the given `audit` module, and no CAT policy states it as a need. A story for it would invent a trace. If the trader needs it, P0 adds a POL in a later version.
traces: POL-CAT-001

DECISION: Excluded scope is recorded in business-policies and produces no DEFERRED stories
Accepted. Pre-assembled kits, activity-specific catalogue models and the multi-vendor catalogue are decided exclusions with activation triggers in business-policies SCOPE EXCEPTIONS. Engine §4 reserves DEFERRED for items the user will not decide. The DEFERRED table points to the exclusions.
traces: POL-CAT-001, POL-CAT-002
