## OVERVIEW
A multi-tenant SaaS for small and medium retail and wholesale traders in Egypt and the Gulf. One product covers the back-office (purchasing, inventory, sales, receivables/payables, accounting, tax & e-invoicing), an offline-capable point of sale and an integrated storefront, all on **one catalogue, one stock and one customer base** [KB:raw-idea.md §1]. Mandatory e-invoicing (ZATCA phase 2, Egypt's B2C e-receipt from Sept 2025) drives adoption [KB:raw-idea.md §2]. Behaviour varies by trading activity only through six capability flags set by activity presets, never by the activity name [KB:raw-idea.md §3 D2, §4]. Everything `erp-core` 1.6.0 provides is used, not rebuilt: tenancy, users and the customer realm, settings, lookups, files, numbering, events, audit, notifications, printing, module entitlements and, new in 1.6.0, the general ledger. The 14 business modules form a downward-only graph whose two roots are Catalog (items) and Party (customers and suppliers) [KB:raw-idea.md §5].

## Open points: PROPOSALS for round 2

**PROPOSAL: Use the profile codes SLS, SWR and SFR; retire SAL, SRW and STF**
- **Affects:** module codes, IDs, steering. Closes OI-01, and P-01 together with bounded contexts as DP §7.2.
- **Options:**
  - A) Profile codes. They are already durable, and the engine's own detection table uses them.
  - B) Domain-profile aliases. This needs a profile change and re-minting of every alias.
- **Researched:** The registry §2.2 and E2 forbid minting IDs with the aliases. The engine §2.1 table lists SLS, SWR and SFR.
- **Recommended:** A.
- **traces:** OI-01, OI-04

**PROPOSAL: Re-match erp-core 1.6.0; add `gl` as a given; the P-03 seams stay platform-own**
- **Affects:** ACC, STL, given rows. Closes OI-02 and OI-06.
- **Researched:** The 1.6.0 manifest table lists `gl` (chart of accounts, fiscal periods, journal entries, trial balance; GlPostingApi). It also lists report `payment-file-export`. No entry provides custom attributes, lot, dual quantity, cash & bank or a tax adapter.
- **Recommended:**
  - CAND-ACC-002 GL account, CAND-ACC-003 journal entry and CAND-ACC-006 accounting period move to the given and get no entity IDs.
  - ACC keeps posting rules, cost centres, budgets and accounting mode.
  - The P-03 seams stay platform-own in CAT, INV, STL and TAX.
- **traces:** OI-02, OI-06, CAND-ACC-002, CAND-ACC-003, CAND-ACC-006

**PROPOSAL: Capability flags and activity presets are given `cu` settings; no new module**
- **Affects:** OI-05, CAND-OPEN-003, CAND-OPEN-004.
- **Options:**
  - A) A new `SET` module. It is not in `module_prefixes`, so a profile change is needed.
  - B) Given `cu` with a FeatureFlags read facade (no entities). Each setup-wizard step goes to its owner: tenant (company), `cu` (preset), TAX, STL (payment methods), CAT (item import).
- **Researched:** The profile rule says "activity presets and capability flags are core settings, read through FeatureFlags". `cu` provides `platform-defaults` and `tenant-overrides`.
- **Recommended:** B.
- **traces:** OI-05, CAND-OPEN-003, CAND-OPEN-004

**PROPOSAL: One Party identity with Customer and Supplier roles**
- **Affects:** PTY entities.
- **Options:**
  - A) Two separate entities. This is simplest, but a dual-role trader is entered twice.
  - B) One Party with roles. The tax identity and addresses are held once, and AR/AP netting stays possible later.
- **Researched:** SAP S/4HANA's Business Partner (customer and vendor integration), Odoo `res.partner` and the Dynamics 365 global address book all use a single party identity. This comes from general knowledge, not live research this round. Wholesale counterparties often both buy and sell.
- **Recommended:** B. The UI still shows only "Customer" and "Supplier", so G7 is unaffected.
- **traces:** CAND-PTY-001, CAND-PTY-002

**PROPOSAL: `sec` owns the account; PTY owns the commercial customer; matching guests are linked by invitation**
- **Affects:** OI-13, POL-PTY-002.
- **Recommended:**
  - Customer → CUSTOMER-realm user is a 0..1 SOFT-READ link.
  - On a first order, a guest whose contact matches an existing Customer gets a `sec` customer invitation and is linked when they accept. Otherwise a new Customer is created.
- **Reason:** This keeps POL-PTY-001 (one customer base) without linking anyone through an unverified contact.
- **traces:** OI-13, CAND-PTY-001, POL-PTY-001, POL-PTY-002

**PROPOSAL: PTY owns the credit limit and payment terms; STL contributes exposure through a PTY extension point**
- **Affects:** OI-16, CAND-PTY-004 (split), CAND-STL-003 (withdrawn), the DAG.
- **Options:**
  - A) STL owns the limit. SLS (tier 3) could not read it without an upward edge.
  - B) PTY owns the limit, and PTY subscribes to STL events. That is also an upward edge.
  - C) PTY owns the limit and terms and declares an exposure extension point that STL contributes, following the same contributor pattern erp-core uses (TenantProvisioningContributor, PermissionContributor).
- **Recommended:** C. Supplier payment terms are flag-independent.
- **traces:** OI-16, CAND-PTY-004, CAND-STL-003, POL-PTY-003

**PROPOSAL: Credit overrun is a tenant setting; default "block with override by a permitted role"**
- **Options:** A) hard block · B) warn only · C) block with override · D) a `cu` setting offering B or C, default C.
- **Researched:** "Nothing imposed" [KB §6.2], "every error offers a next action" [KB §6.5], role presets [KB §3 D8].
- **Recommended:** D. On acceptance, the next free POL id (POL-PTY-008) records it in EARS "unwanted" form.
- **traces:** POL-PTY-003

**PROPOSAL: Price lists follow customer groups; add a PRC → PTY SOFT-READ edge**
- **Researched:** `b2bCredit` covers "price lists" for wholesale customers [KB §4.1]. A price list is "for a channel or customer group" [DP §7.1]. PTY owns the `customer-group` lookup.
- **Recommended:** Add the edge. It extends the fixed graph without contradicting it, and PRC stays tier 2.
- **traces:** CAND-PRC-001, customer-group

**PROPOSAL: Seed one walk-in customer (عميل نقدي) per tenant**
- **Options:**
  - A) POS invoices without a party.
  - B) One seeded default customer, created through TenantProvisioningContributor.
- **Researched:** Single-default dimensions [KB §6.8]; a new employee sells within 10 minutes [KB §6.10]. Saudi simplified invoices are believed not to need buyer identification. This is not verified this round.
- **Recommended:** B. Every invoice then has a party for ageing and reports. On acceptance, the next free POL id records it.
- **traces:** CAND-PTY-001

**PROPOSAL: Edge kinds as DP §6; party data reaches TAX and SHP on the document snapshot**
- **Recommended:**
  - Edges stated in KB §5 are HARD-FK. ACC, APR, SFR and INS are SOFT-READ.
  - SLS and PUR documents carry a snapshot of the party data. TAX reads it for e-invoice buyer data, and SHP for the delivery address. Neither gets a direct edge to PTY.
- **Reason:** The snapshot also keeps issued legal documents unchanged when party data is later edited.
- **traces:** OI-17
