**What's in the SRS**
- **Counts:** 109 requirements, 123 acceptance criteria, 5 entities, 33 business rules, 5 screen requirements. All 17 PRD stories are covered and every story has at least one "If …, then" requirement.
- **Entities:** Party, Customer, Supplier, Address and Credit override request. The module consumes nothing from other business modules (`consumes: []`).
- **Lookups:** CUSTOMER_GROUP, ADDRESS_TYPE, CREDIT_OVERRUN_ACTION, CREDIT_CHECK_RESULT, CREDIT_OVERRIDE_STATUS.
- **erp-core usage:** 10 rows (sec ×3, cu, mdl, sequence, tenant, audit, events, notif), each `via` copied from the brief's manifest list.
- **`b2bCredit` coverage:** a table maps each activity's preset value to the flag-on and flag-off requirements.

**Main decisions (ADRs)**
- **ADR-014:** a counterparty is held once as a Party, with a Customer role, a Supplier role or both. Credit limit and payment terms (in days) are Customer fields owned by PTY, which settles the registry's open question OI-16 for v1.
- **ADR-015:** a storefront guest is matched to an existing customer by email first, then by mobile if exactly one active party matches. Accounts are created only through an erp-core invitation and linked when the shopper accepts it. Guest details never overwrite an existing customer.
- **ADR-016:** credit overrun approval is a PTY-owned "credit override request" with the states PENDING, APPROVED, REJECTED, CANCELLED and USED. The Owner preset holds the approval right (UPDATE on page `PTY_CREDIT_OVERRIDE`).
- **ADR-017:** a customer's open exposure is the sum of amounts reported by downstream modules (0 if none are registered). A customer with no credit limit gets the result NO_CREDIT. PTY answers the "can `b2bCredit` be switched off" check for whichever module ends up owning the flags.

**Things that might trip the analyzer**
- **Unverified module key:** in the erp-core usage block I wrote module keys as `modules.sec`, `modules.cu`, and so on, following the brief's `erp-core:modules.x` notation. I didn't check them against `core-manifest.yaml` because the brief limits me to its own contents. If the manifest uses short keys, check C-GIVEN-2 will flag this.
- **Glossary hit:** the term "customer group" (مجموعة العملاء) comes from the approved PRD and registry, but the glossary lists "group" and مجموعة as words to avoid. That may raise a minor C5.17 finding.
- **Module registry not read directly:** I didn't open the module registry or business policies. They exist in the repo but weren't in the brief, so I cite them only as quoted in the PRD.
- **Assumed timeout:** the 3-second limit for exposure reporting is my own default, recorded in ADR-017. The next stage (P3.1) can change it.
