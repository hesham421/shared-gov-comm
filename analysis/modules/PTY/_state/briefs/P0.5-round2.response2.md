DECISION: Story priority follows the KB §8 delivery waves, and "—" applies wherever no wave is named
- Reasoning: I accept option B. KB §3 D10 makes the delivery order binding, so wave membership clearly implies priority. Wave 1 is Catalog, Party, Inventory, Purchasing, Sales & POS, Settlements and Tax. Wave 2 is Storefront and Shipping.
- Amendment: round 1 gave customer groups (US-PTY-006) HIGH, but that is not clearly implied.
  - Its consumer is Pricing, which KB §8 does not place in any wave. Wave 1 lists neither Pricing nor price lists.
  - Its source is DP §7.1 and platform decision 8, which carry no ordering.
  - Under the engine rule "only if stated or clearly implied", it takes "—", the same as US-PTY-017 (an AUTO-only need).
- Final rule:
  - A need served by Party's wave-1 consumers (back-office, POS) is HIGH.
  - A storefront-only need is MEDIUM.
  - A need sourced only from an AUTO decision, or whose consumer no wave names, is "—".
  - The result is 13 HIGH, 2 MEDIUM (US-PTY-009, US-PTY-010) and 2 "—" (US-PTY-006, US-PTY-017).
- traces: US-PTY-001…US-PTY-017; KB §3 D10, §8; engine §2

DECISION: Addresses and customer groups are stories under POL-PTY-001, and supplier payment terms stay registry scope with no story
- Reasoning: I accept option B.
  - Addresses are named in KB §5 #4, and the registry makes them SHARED with SLS, PUR and SFR. Customer groups are shared classification per DP §7.1 and decision 8. Both serve the one-customer-base policy.
  - Supplier payment terms have no policy. Tracing them to POL-PTY-006 (quick registration) or POL-PTY-003 (customer credit under `b2bCredit`) would misstate those policies, because supplier terms are flag-independent (registry AUTO).
  - The TRACES RULE says a need that serves no policy "is scope" and stays in the registry. P1 derives it from the Payment term entity, so no P0 re-opening is needed and nothing is deferred.
- traces: US-PTY-005, US-PTY-006, POL-PTY-001, POL-PTY-006; engine §2 TRACES RULE, §3

DECISION: Global search covers customers only in v1
- Reasoning: I accept option A. KB §6.6 lists "items, customers, documents" and POL-PTY-007 says "every Customer". Supplier purchase documents are already findable as documents. A supplier-search story would have no source and would need a new P0 policy.
- traces: US-PTY-007, POL-PTY-007; KB §6.6
