## Decision
Accepted. Option A would discard real signal in §4.2 and §8. The rule is applied the same way to every story:
- **HIGH:** the §6 simplicity requirements (made measurable by D9), the fixed decision D2, the §2 e-invoicing driver, the differentiator of §1, and variants and bundles (required by the first two launch presets in §4.2).
- **MEDIUM:** storefront-facing stories, because the storefront goes live in wave 2 (§8.2).
- **LOW:** weighed items, because produce and meat by weight is not in the launch priority of §4.2.
- **"—":** everything else.
Priority orders attention inside CAT only. Per D10, CAT ships whole in wave 1.
