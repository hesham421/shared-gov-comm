## Decision
Reasoning : I accept option A and amend the wording.
- KB §6.10 is the only stated metric. It measures the whole setup and first invoice, not one story.
- So US-INV-004, US-INV-009 and US-INV-012 name it as the shared metric they contribute to. Every other story keeps "—".
- The "≤ 10 minutes for a new employee" metric belongs to SLS selling.
- The "≤ 4 mandatory fields" in KB §6.1 is a constraint for P1, not a story metric.
